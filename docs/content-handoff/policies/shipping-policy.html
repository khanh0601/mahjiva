Order processing

Orders are hand-inspected and prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed.

Orders placed after 12:00 PM EST or on weekends/public holidays begin processing on the following business day.

Where your order ships from

Mahjiva fulfils orders from more than one warehouse. Depending on which location holds stock when you order, your parcel is dispatched either from our fulfilment partner in California, United States, or from our overseas atelier.

The warehouse is assigned automatically and cannot be selected at checkout. Orders dispatched from our overseas atelier take longer to arrive, and the delivery windows below reflect that difference.

Delivery Time

Delivery time is counted from the date your parcel is dispatched, not from the date you place your order.

United States and Canada:

  • Dispatched from our US warehouse: arrives in 3–7 business days after dispatch (typically 1–2 weeks total arrival window).
  • Dispatched from our overseas atelier: arrives in 7–12 business days after dispatch (typically 2–3 weeks total arrival window).

Across all fulfillment locations, parcels arrive within 1–3 weeks of your order.

Australia and other international destinations:

  • Arrives 10–25 business days after dispatch, depending on destination and customs clearance.

These are estimates based on our own delivery records, not guarantees. Delivery may take longer during peak seasons, or where a parcel is held for customs inspection or delayed by the carrier.

If your order has not arrived within 30 business days of dispatch, contact us and we will reship it or refund you in full, at your choice.

We do not offer overnight, express or guaranteed next-day delivery.

Shipping options at checkout

  • Complimentary Standard Insured Shipping — Free on all orders. Every order is hand-inspected, packed in protective packaging, and dispatched within 1–2 business days with full end-to-end tracking provided at no extra cost.

End-to-end tracking & carrier updates

You receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is dispatched. When parcels transition from our overseas atelier to domestic carriers such as USPS, both reference numbers automatically synchronize under your single tracking link. Carrier scans typically begin updating within 2–4 business days after dispatch.

If your order has not arrived

Please email care@mahjiva.com with your order number before taking any other step. We reply within 24 hours and will:

  • Trace the parcel with the carrier, or
  • Reship your order at no cost, or
  • Refund you in full

— whichever you prefer, for any order past the delivery window above.

Our client care commitment: Reaching out directly to our concierge team at care@mahjiva.com ensures an immediate, hassle-free resolution within 24 hours. We arrange a fast replacement or full refund directly, ensuring a smooth luxury experience without third-party delays.

Cancellations and address changes

You may cancel an order or correct the delivery address within 12 hours of placing it, by emailing care@mahjiva.com with your order number. After 12 hours the order may already be in preparation at the warehouse and can no longer be changed or cancelled.

Want to add items rather than cancel? See Add to your order within 24 hours above.

If a parcel is returned to us because the address given at checkout was incomplete or incorrect, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Reshipping costs may apply.

Proof of delivery

Our carriers record a delivery scan, and in many cases a photograph of the parcel at the delivery location together with GPS coordinates. Once a carrier records a parcel as delivered to the address given at checkout, Mahjiva is not responsible for parcels subsequently stolen or moved. If a parcel shows as delivered but you cannot find it, contact us within 7 days and we will open a carrier trace on your behalf.

Damaged items

Report damage to care@mahjiva.com within 30 days of delivery, with photographs or video of the item and its packaging. For damage that occurred in transit, please tell us within 7 days of delivery where possible, so we can open a claim with the carrier. We replace or refund damaged items at no cost to you, as set out in our Refund Policy.

Incorrect addresses

We are not responsible for non-delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address given at checkout. Please check your address before completing your order, and contact us within 12 hours if you need to correct it.

Shipping destinations

We ship worldwide. Delivery times by region are listed above. Orders to some remote destinations may take longer, and any duties vary by country.

Customs and duties

Customers outside the United States are responsible for import duties, taxes and handling fees charged by their own country. These are not included in the price paid to us and we cannot estimate them in advance.
